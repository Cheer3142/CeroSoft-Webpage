# Design System: CeroSoft

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Live Twin"**

CeroSoft presents itself the way an operations product would, not the way a studio would: as a cinematic B2B product site in the grammar of Terminal Industries (user-pinned reference), translated into CeroSoft's own brand. The real operation becomes a digital twin. The hero is a full-bleed charcoal stage where a three.js wireframe of Punna Residence 5 draws itself from the ground up, slowly orbits, and lights cyan live markers at the rooms where CondoMate events happen. Everything that follows is product proof, not adjectives, and every product screen is honestly labelled as demo data.

The page breathes in long alternating takes: charcoal cinematic grounds for the twin, services with the process, and the closing; light fog-grey (#F4F5F5) sections for statements, calculators, the CondoMate walkthrough, the founder, FAQ and contact. The two grounds meet at a trapezoid notch, a shallow tab of the light ground that bites into the dark edge, so a section change reads as a cut rather than a stripe. Section heads alternate between centred and split (heading left, lead right) so the page does not open every chapter the same way. Statements are set big, light and tight in Inter 400; Thai carries the same scale in Noto Sans Thai 500 so vowels and tone marks stay clear. Geist Mono appears only as small uppercase Latin data labels, the instrument readout of the world.

One accent does all the signalling. Cero Cyan marks the primary action, every live event, and the last letter of the typed hero headline. Nowhere else. A muted amber exists for one job only: the "Demo data" label beside product screenshots. Density is low; the light sections are mostly air with a few 14px cards; the dark panels carry one clipped corner as their signature. A person is always one tap from a phone call. The previous drafting-sheet world ("The Drawing Set") and the generic dark-SaaS-cards template are both rejected.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Charcoal cinematic grounds alternating with fog-light statement sections, joined by trapezoid notches.
- A single accent, Cero Cyan, reserved for action, live events and the typed headline's last letter.
- Large, tight Inter 400 statements; Noto Sans Thai 500 for Thai; Geist Mono uppercase micro-labels for Latin data only.
- Floating capsule nav with a cyan call-to-action cell, a cyan phone chip on phones, and 44px touch chips below 1080px.
- Centred and split section heads alternate; dense content is set as scannable lists, not card grids.
- 14px card radii; dark cards cut with one 26px clipped corner.
- Real product screens with an amber "Demo data" label set beside them, never over them.
- A lazy-loaded wireframe digital twin with cyan live markers; reduced motion shows the finished frame.

## Colors

A near-monochrome charcoal-and-fog system with one cool cyan voice and one amber honesty label.

### Primary
- **Cero Cyan** (#00ADB5): the brand accent. Primary buttons, the nav "talk to us" cell and phone chip, live-dot pulses and twin markers, filled range tracks, focus rings on dark grounds, text selection, links that act (founder link, the phone number in a failed-send message), the leading edge of the twin's draw-in, and the last letter of the hero headline. Text on cyan is always Charcoal, never white. A 12% cyan wash is allowed only behind a live dot (the founder availability pill).
- **Cero Cyan Lift** (#22C4CC): hover state of cyan fills only.

### Tertiary
- **Demo Amber** (#FFF3D6 fill, #6B4A00 ink, #F0D58C border): the "Demo data" label on light grounds. On charcoal it becomes a 14% amber wash (rgba(255,214,120,0.14)) with #FFD98A ink and a 35% amber border. It exists only to mark product screenshots as demo data.

### Neutral
- **Charcoal** (#222831): the cinematic ground (hero, services, footer, dark cards) and the ink colour on light grounds. One value, two roles.
- **Charcoal Raised** (#2A3039): a lifted dark surface (declared as a token; the services section no longer uses a raised panel, so it has no live surface right now).
- **Charcoal Deep** (#1D2229) and **Hero Glow** (#2E3641): the hero's radial vignette ends, darker at the edges and slightly lifted behind the twin.
- **Device Black** (#10141A): phone bezels around real CondoMate screens.
- **Graphite** (#393E46): secondary text on light (bullets, values line, bridge lines), diagram strokes, scrollbar thumb.
- **Fog Light** (#F4F5F5): the light section ground and page background.
- **Paper** (#FFFFFF): light cards, tabs and pills that sit on Fog Light.
- **Diagram Well** (#EEF0F1): the inset well behind path diagrams and the tag fill.
- **Muted** (#5B616B): body copy and descriptions on light grounds.
- **Step Idle** (#7D838B): CondoMate step headings before they come into focus (large text only).
- **Reveal Start** (#A2A8AE): the transient start tint of the scroll-scrubbed statement reveal; never a resting text colour.
- **Line** (#E1E4E6) and **Line Strong** (#C9CDD1): card borders and dividers; Line Strong for list rules (FAQ, the call block above the phone button).
- **On-dark text tiers**, brightest to quietest: **On-Dark Heading** (#F3F4F5) for headlines and the closing; **On-Dark** (#ECEEF0) for default text; **On-Dark Soft** (#C9CED3) for longer reading copy and links on charcoal (founder text and experience roles, nav links, footer links, the hero lead); **On-Dark Muted** (#A9AFB6) for leads, labels, field labels, counts and secondary lines. **On-Dark Line** (rgba(236,238,240,0.14)) for hairlines.
- **Danger** (#A43232) / **Danger on Dark** (#FF9D9D): form validation and send failure only. The live form sits on a dark card, so Danger on Dark is the one in use.

Several near-variants of On-Dark Soft are in the build as drift (#D9DDE1 nav links, #BCC2C8 hero lead, #C3C8CE footer links, #CFD4D9 dark tabs, #D6DADE service pills); new work uses On-Dark Soft.

### Named Rules
**The One Voice Rule.** Cero Cyan means "act here" or "this is live." It never fills a section, a card background or decorative ornament; if a cyan element is neither an action nor a live event (or the headline's last letter), it is wrong.

**The Charcoal Is Ink Rule.** The dark ground and the light-ground text are the same Charcoal. Do not introduce a second near-black.

**The Demo Label Rule.** Amber appears only as the "Demo data" label, and the label sits beside a product screenshot (in its caption or panel label), never over the UI it describes. One label per area: the CondoMate panel, the first phone in the stacked mobile walkthrough, the console caption.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Inter Variable (with Noto Sans Thai, system-ui fallback)
**Body Font:** Inter Variable for Latin; Noto Sans Thai for Thai (the site switches the whole stack under `lang="th"`)
**Label/Mono Font:** Geist Mono (with ui-monospace)

**Character:** Inter at large size, regular weight and tight negative tracking gives calm, expensive product statements; Noto Sans Thai takes over at weight 500 with almost no tracking and generous leading so Thai reads cleanly; Geist Mono is the quiet instrument voice for data.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (400, clamp(3.1rem, 7vw, 6rem), 1.02, -0.04em): the typed hero headline only. Thai: 500, clamp(2.6rem, 6vw, 5.2rem), 1.25. On phones the hero drops to clamp(2.7rem, 13vw, 3.6rem) Latin and clamp(2.2rem, 10.5vw, 3rem) Thai.
- **Headline** (400, clamp(2.1rem, 4.2vw, 3.75rem), 1.08, -0.035em, balanced wrap): section titles. Thai: 500, clamp(1.85rem, 3.6vw, 3.2rem), 1.3.
- **Figure** (400, clamp(2.4rem, 4.6vw, 3.8rem), 1.1, tabular numerals): calculated results on dark cards.
- **Title Large** (400, clamp(1.7rem, 2.8vw, 2.5rem), 1.15, -0.03em): CondoMate step heads, and the family for console, founder and process sub-heads. Thai switches to 500 with 0 tracking.
- **Statement** (400, clamp(1.2rem, 2.1vw, 1.75rem), 1.5): the word-by-word reveal paragraph, max 34em. Thai: line-height 1.7, 0 tracking.
- **Title** (500, ~1.2 to 1.4rem, 1.3, -0.015em): card headings.
- **Title Small** (1.15rem, 1.3): service item titles at 500; the founder's name, the call block title and the phone button number at 600; the footer tagline at 400.
- **Body** (400, 1rem, 1.6; Thai 1.75) and **Body Lead** (1.05rem): copy, held to 40 to 64ch.
- **Body Small** (400, 0.9rem, 1.5): form field labels, the contact hint, process stage text.
- **Nav Link** (400, 0.875rem): capsule navigation links.
- **Caption** (0.82rem): data notes, the copyright line.
- **Badge** (Inter 600, 0.75rem, 0.01em, no case transform): the "Demo data" label. It is a sans label so it reads as plain language in Thai and English.
- **Label** (Geist Mono 400, 0.72rem, 0.06em, uppercase, tabular): tags, stage numbers, panel counts and place label, footer heads, nav chips. In Thai, labels switch to Noto Sans Thai 0.8rem, no tracking, no case transform.

### Named Rules
**The Mono Is Latin Data Rule.** Geist Mono is used only for Latin data and UI chips: counts, numbers, short codes. Thai text is never set in mono and never uppercased or tracked.

**The Light Statement Rule.** Big Latin statements are weight 400 with negative tracking. Do not set Latin display or headline text bold.

**The Typed Last Letter Rule.** The hero headline types in grapheme by grapheme (Thai vowels and tone marks stay attached) and its last letter grapheme is Cero Cyan. No other headline carries a coloured letter.

## Layout

Single column of full-bleed sections. Content sits in a centred wrap of min(1240px, 100% - 2 × gutter) (narrow variant 980px), with a fluid gutter of clamp(16px, 4vw, 48px). Sections are separated by a section rhythm of clamp(96px, 11vw, 168px); consecutive light sections drop their top padding so the light ground runs continuously.

Section heads come in two forms. **Centred heads** (max 860 to 980px) put a 60ch lead under the headline; every paragraph in a centred head is held to 60ch. **Split heads** put the headline left and the lead right (1.1fr / 1fr, bottom-aligned, column gap clamp(32px, 6vw, 96px), lead max 52ch). The page alternates them: paths centred, calculator split, CondoMate centred, services split (on dark), about centred.

Grids are simple: three path cards; a two-column calculator (form left, dark result right); a two-column CondoMate scroller (text blocks left, sticky dark product panel right); a services list five across (title plus one line, no pills) with a five-step process strip under a hairline in the same dark section; the about section as a centred head, a single centred values line ("Simple · Practical · Reliable · ..."), and the founder card; two-column FAQ and contact splits, with a call block under the contact copy. Card gaps are 16px; column gaps in split layouts are clamp(32px, 6vw, 96px). The internal spacing rhythm steps 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48px.

Responsive: at 1080px the nav links collapse into a menu toggle, the capsule grows to 56px and every nav chip becomes a 44px touch target; at 1000px every split (including split heads) goes to one column, the services list and process strip drop to two columns, the sticky CondoMate panel is replaced by an inline phone under each step (the first one carries the demo label above it), and the hero scrim turns bottom-up; at 640px the nav CTA cell hides and the cyan phone chip appears, hero buttons go full width, services and process go to one column (process rows as number, title, text), console tabs grow to 44px, the notch shortens to 18px and the footer stacks. The hero twin moves from right-of-centre (wide) to the upper band (phone), where only the first marker shows its label. Focusable form fields keep a 110px scroll margin so focus never hides under the fixed nav.

### Named Rules
**The Alternating Heads Rule.** Do not open two consecutive sections with the same head form; alternate centred and split.

**The List Over Cards Rule.** When a section holds five or more parallel items (services, process steps), set them as a hairline-ruled list, not a grid of boxes.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from ground changes and cinematic light, not from shadow stacks. Cards are flat with a 1px border on light ground; dark surfaces separate by tone and hairlines. The hero gets depth from a radial glow behind the twin, a left-and-bottom scrim that protects the headline, and depth fog in the wireframe shader. The nav capsule floats with a translucent blur over the hero, turns solid once the hero has scrolled away, and stays solid for the rest of the page (it returns to translucent only when scrolling back into the hero).

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Device drop** (`box-shadow: 0 40px 80px -30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`): the phone bezel inside the dark CondoMate panel.
- **Console lift** (`box-shadow: 0 30px 60px -36px rgba(34, 40, 49, 0.45)`): the management-console screenshot frame on light ground.
- **Marker halo** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 173, 181, 0.25)`): ring around a live twin marker dot.
- **Thumb** (`box-shadow: 0 2px 6px rgba(34, 40, 49, 0.18)`): range slider thumb.

### Named Rules
**The Product Floats, Cards Don't Rule.** Only real product imagery (phone, console screenshot) casts a shadow. Text cards are flat.

## Shapes

Gently rounded and engineered. Cards are 14px; buttons and form fields 8px; chips, tabs, tags and marker labels 6px; the nav capsule and mobile menu 12px; diagram wells and the founder portrait 10px; feature pills, the demo label and the availability pill fully round. Dark cards (calculator result, CondoMate panel, contact form, founder card) keep their 14px radius on three corners and cut the top-right corner with a 26px diagonal clip, the system's signature silhouette. Section joins use a trapezoid notch: a centred tab min(520px, 64%) wide and 26px tall (18px on phones) with 5% sloped shoulders, coloured as the light ground, sitting on the dark section's top or bottom edge. Live dots and markers are circles with an expanding pulse ring.

## Components

### Buttons
Confident and quiet: solid, square-ish, with a trailing arrow that nudges 3px on hover.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (8px), 48px minimum height, 20px side padding, 600 weight at 0.95rem.
- **Primary:** Cero Cyan fill, Charcoal text; hover lifts to Cero Cyan Lift.
- **Ghost (on dark):** transparent, 1px rgba(255,255,255,0.32) border, white text; hover solid white border and a 6% white wash.
- **Outline (on light):** 1px Charcoal border and text; hover inverts to Charcoal fill, white text.
- **Call:** the outline button carrying a leading phone icon and the number at 1.15rem, tabular, 52px tall; the icon does not nudge. It sits in a call block (title, button, short note) under a Line Strong rule in the contact column.
- **Focus:** 2px outline offset 3px, Charcoal on light, Cero Cyan on dark. Disabled drops to 60% opacity.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** alternate charcoal (#222831) and fog-light (#F4F5F5) grounds and join them with the trapezoid notch.
- **Do** keep Cero Cyan (#00ADB5) to primary actions, live events and the hero headline's last letter, with Charcoal text on it.
- **Do** set Latin statements in Inter 400 with negative tracking, and Thai headings in Noto Sans Thai 500 with near-zero tracking and at least 1.25 line height.
- **Do** use Geist Mono uppercase (0.72rem, 0.06em) only for Latin data labels; switch to Noto Sans Thai, no case, no tracking, in Thai.
- **Do** give light cards a 14px radius and a 1px Line border; give dark cards the single 26px clipped corner.
- **Do** show real CondoMate screens and the wireframe twin as proof; give every animation a reduced-motion state that shows the finished result.
- **Do** label every product-screenshot area once with the amber "Demo data" pill, set beside the screenshot.
- **Do** keep a tap-to-call path visible at every width: the call block in contact, the phone row in the mobile menu, the phone chip on phones, the footer link.
- **Do** make touch controls at least 44px below 1080px, and give each invalid form field its own message in the page language.
- **Do** alternate centred and split section heads, and set five or more parallel items as a ruled list.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use cyan as a section fill, card background, gradient or decoration.
- **Don't** use amber for anything except the demo label, and don't lay the label over product UI.
- **Don't** set Thai in mono, uppercase or tracked type.
- **Don't** bold Latin display or headline text.
- **Don't** add shadows to text cards; only product imagery floats.
- **Don't** return to the drafting-sheet look of the rejected "Drawing Set" world (title blocks, sheet borders, dimension lines) or to a generic grid of glowing dark SaaS cards.
- **Don't** use stock photography; illustrate with real UI, the twin, or simple system diagrams.
- **Don't** leave Reveal Start grey (#A2A8AE) as resting text; it is a scroll transition start only.
- **Don't** add another on-dark grey; pick from On-Dark Heading, On-Dark, On-Dark Soft and On-Dark Muted.
